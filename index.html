Hiring Process

1

Application Submission

Submit your CV through our careers page. You will receive an automatic confirmation email once your application is received.

2

First Interview

An introductory call with our HR team to discuss your background, motivation and availability - online or in person, depending on the role.

3

Final Interview

Final discussion with the Store Manager or Area Manager regarding culture fit, goals and expectations.

4

Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive an official offer and begin the onboarding process with the Cosmos Sport team.
